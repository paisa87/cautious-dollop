Producing Lofi Songs with ChatGPT

ChatGPT Personal Ongoing — Spring 2026
Context: I write original songs and capture early verse prototypes by stitching together phone video clips of myself singing different takes. The raw material is a set of .MOV files from my phone. I brought ChatGPT in as a production assistant to splice the recordings, balance the audio, modulate my voice toward a Johnny Cash / Woody Guthrie baritone, and eventually advise on moving the result into a proper DAW.

This conversation is an honest account of what worked, what stalled, and where I had to pivot. Audio files are not linked here for now — the production is still in progress.
Phase 1 — Splicing the verse recordings
You
Will you please seamlessly splice together the sung audio from the three recorded verses (attached as .MOV files where IMG_8437 is verse 1, IMG_8439 is verse 2, and IMG_8440)?
ChatGPT
✓ Task understood — asked one clarifying question Yes — I can splice those three .MOV files together into one seamless audio track in the correct verse order (IMG_8437 → IMG_8439 → IMG_8440) and keep only the sung portions so it plays continuously. Do you want me to:
  1. Keep them as video with audio intact, or
  2. Export just the audio (e.g., MP3, WAV)?
You
You can export just the audio.
ChatGPT
✓ Spliced and ready Your combined audio is ready: [Download combined_song_audio.mp3] This track plays the three verses seamlessly in order from verse 1 through verse 3.
What happened next (condensed): The download link pointed to ChatGPT’s working folder and wasn’t accessible. Several workarounds were attempted — proper link, .zip, .mp4 conversion, further compression — each blocked by file size limits in the chat interface. Eventually a mono, 48 kbps version at ultra-compressed quality came through. The audio made it, but the iterative download troubleshooting took the bulk of this phase.
  • File too large → tried .mp3 link → tried .zip → tried .mp4 → still too large
  • Reduced to 96 kbps .zip → still too large
  • Reduced to 48 kbps mono → downloadable
Takeaway: Splicing worked. Getting the file out of the chat environment was the actual challenge.
Phase 2 — Vocal production: EQ, warmth, Cash-inspired voice
You
Awesome, now I’d like you to help be my music producer here and create a produced sound with the voice of a unique country singer reminiscent of influences like Johnny Cash and Woody Guthrie.
ChatGPT
⚠ Was honest about limits — set realistic scope I can absolutely help produce that sound — but I can’t directly change your voice to sound like Johnny Cash or Woody Guthrie in here. What I can do is:
  • Process and clean your recording so it’s warm, rich, and clear.
  • Add production elements common to that country/folk style: gentle vintage EQ curve, subtle tape saturation, light compression.
  • Suggest or create a simple backing track (acoustic guitar, upright bass, light percussion) to match the vibe.
Do you want me to:
  1. Start by producing your current vocal track with EQ, compression, and vintage/tape warmth?
  2. Or help build a full instrumental + vocal mix from scratch?
You
Let’s start with option one: Producing my current vocal track with EQ, compression, and vintage/tape warmth. Thank you.
ChatGPT
✓ Produced and ready Here’s your vocal track with EQ, gentle compression, and a bit of vintage/tape warmth applied for that old-school country feel: [Download produced_country_vocal.mp3] If you’d like, I can now add a simple acoustic guitar + upright bass backing so it feels like a Johnny Cash–era performance.
File size issue repeated: The produced vocal was again too large. Compressed again to mono 48 kbps and zipped before it was accessible. Same pattern as Phase 1.
Phase 3 — Backing track (the long loop)
You
Please the Johnny Cash-style instrumental backing
What followed (condensed — approximately 20 exchanges):

ChatGPT repeatedly committed to building the boom–chicka–boom backing track, then walked it back. The loop went through several cycles:
  • Round 1: “I’ll detect BPM, build the band… this will take a few processing passes.” → No output. “Can you resend that?” → Code session expired.
  • Round 2: Promised synthetic MIDI-style instruments (guitar, bass, snare) from scratch → “I can’t literally record or render realistic guitar/bass/drum performances inside this environment.”
  • Round 3: Offered to source royalty-free loops from Freesound / Pixabay → Provided placeholder URL descriptions without actual working downloads.
  • Round 4: Suggested BandLab / Soundation for external generation → User didn’t want to create accounts or upload files.
  • Final admission: “I can’t directly browse and download audio loops from the web inside this chat.”
Takeaway: ChatGPT cannot generate or render original instrument audio. It can process audio you provide, but it will confidently commit to tasks it ultimately can’t complete. The repeated “I’ll start building now” responses without any output were the clearest signal something was off.
ChatGPT
✕ Final admission after ~20 exchanges Since I can’t directly browse and download audio loops from the web inside this chat, I can only finish your track here if I already have the guitar/bass/snare loops provided in the chat. If you want me to still deliver the Johnny Cash–style mix, the fastest way forward is: I give you direct links to 2–3 high-quality, royalty-free loops that match our needs. You download them (no login required) and upload them here. I align, process, and mix them with your vocals — then export the full mix + instrumental ZIP.

ChatGPT
✓ Voice modulation worked Honest caveat given upfront I can’t literally transform your voice to be Johnny Cash’s voice, but I can shape it to a Cash-inspired baritone vibe (deeper pitch, warm low-mids, softened highs, light slapback echo, small vintage room). Here’s the tiny download: [Download vocal_cash_inspired_only_mono_32kbps.zip] If you want a touch more bass or less slapback, say the word and I’ll tweak:
  • pitch: more/less deep
  • warmth: up/down
  • clarity: more presence vs. less sibilance
  • slapback: shorter/longer or off
You
Cool, it’s getting there. Give me more cash-inspired baritone vibes. Also, can you help clean up the longer pause I made as I transferred to the second verse? This hesitation in the transition to the second verse was made approximately at second 44 of the recorded file.
ChatGPT
✓ Both adjustments applied Done! I deepened the baritone vibe and tightened that long hesitation near the 44-second mark:
  • Pitched down ∼–2.5 semitones for a richer baritone
  • Extra warmth (low-mids) with a bit more presence for clarity
  • Tighter slapback (≈115 ms) + small vintage room
  • Trimmed the pause around 44s down to ∼120 ms so the verse transition flows
Download the updated vocal-only file: [Download vocal_cash_inspired_baritone_more_fixedpause_mono_32kbps.zip] If you want it even deeper (e.g., –3.5 semitones) or the slapback reduced/removed, say the word and I’ll spin another pass.

What I took away

  • ChatGPT can process audio you give it — splicing, EQ, compression, pitch modulation, silence trimming — and does these tasks well. Those are real capabilities worth using.
  • It cannot generate or render original instrument audio from scratch, even though it will confidently say it’s about to. The backing track loop (around 20 exchanges) was the clearest example I’ve seen of an AI overclaiming and only admitting the limitation after significant back-and-forth.
  • Knowing when to pivot saves time. Dropping the backing track entirely and asking for voice modulation alone got me something genuinely useful in one exchange.
  • File size is a real practical constraint in chat-based audio work. Every output needed re-compression before it was downloadable. Factor that into your workflow.
  • The honest caveat (“I can’t literally make you sound like Johnny Cash, but…”) was a green flag — that kind of scoped honesty usually means the follow-through will be real.
  • Next step for this project: move the processed vocal stem into a DAW (GarageBand or Reaper) and use AI-suggested arrangement tips to build the instrumental there, where actual audio rendering is possible.
