Brainstorming Undergraduate Research with ChatGPT

ChatGPT Teaching Lab & Research Spring 2026
Context: I have boxes of silica-dried plant specimens tied to accessioned herbarium records, plus microbiology and molecular lab capabilities (including a KingFisher automated extractor and a qPCR thermocycler). I set up a ChatGPT project with relevant background files and used it to brainstorm research directions with genuine peer-review publication potential — with students as potential coauthors. The conversation evolved from broad ideation into detailed experimental design, SOP scaffolding, and process mapping for course-based lab activities.

Phase 1 — Initial ideation from available specimens
You
I have boxes full of dried plant specimens that have herbarium accessioned samples stored in herbaria. What kind of low hanging fruit can I do in terms of research if I have the lab capabilities for microbiology and molecular research?
ChatGPT
✓ Organized 7 research directions by difficulty, cost, and novelty That’s an excellent starting point — herbarium specimens are a goldmine for low-cost, high-impact research, especially if they’re accessioned and tied to verified species IDs.

1. DNA Recovery & Degradation Studies

  • Compare DNA yield and quality from specimens of different ages, families, and preservation conditions.
  • Assess plastid vs. nuclear DNA preservation (e.g., rbcL vs. ITS regions).
  • Develop optimized extraction protocols for archival material — publishable in Applications in Plant Sciences or Taxon.

2. Endophyte and Microbiome Recovery

  • Test if viable microbes persist in dried tissues decades after collection.
  • 16S/ITS amplicon sequencing to assess historical plant microbiomes.
  • Compare herbarium microbiomes vs. living populations of the same species.

3. Historical Genomics / Phylogeography

  • Compare chloroplast haplotypes between herbarium and modern populations.
  • Detect hybridization or introgression signals using barcoding markers.
  • Study range shifts by correlating historical genotypes with modern geography or climate data.

Also suggested: Plant–Microbe Co-evolution Snapshots, Secondary Metabolite Studies, Environmental/Anthropogenic Signal analysis, and curriculum integration opportunities for BTC181.

ChatGPT asked follow-up questions about specimen age range, collection size, and available equipment before proposing specific project proposals.

You
Yes: 1) Some are from the early 2000s while most are from the 2010s; 2) I’d say I have at least 1000 samples; 3) I have all the equipment you listed including an automated DNA extraction device (KingFisher).
Phase 2 — Three scoped, publishable project proposals
ChatGPT
✓ Three fully scoped projects with design, workflow, budget, and target journals

Project 1 — HerbGen-Preserve: DNA Preservation & Amplification Success

Goal: Quantify how age, tissue type, taxon, and drying method predict DNA yield and PCR success from 2000–2019 specimens (~300 specimens).

  • Stratify by decade, tissue, and 6–8 common families.
  • KingFisher extraction; Qubit QC; PCR for rbcL and ITS2; Sanger subset (n≈60).
  • Logistic regression (PCR success ~ age + tissue + family).
  • Estimated budget: ~$2,700–$3,000.
  • Target: Applications in Plant Sciences, PeerJ, PLOS ONE.

Project 2 — Time-Capsule Endophytes: Can We Revive Dried Sheet Microbes?

Goal: Test viability of culturable fungal/bacterial endophytes from 8–12 species across 2000s–2010s; identify isolates and screen basic antagonism.

  • Surface sterilize; plate on PDA, R2A, TSA; pick morphotypes; Sanger ID (ITS or 16S).
  • Estimated budget: ~$2,000.
  • Target: Microbiology Resource Announcements, Fungal Ecology, Mycologia Notes.

Project 3 — Herb-Microbiome Time Series: Historical Amplicons (16S & ITS2)

Goal: Compare microbial community composition on herbarium tissues between 2000s vs. 2010s across taxa/habitats (96-plex MiSeq pilot).

  • 24 host species × 2 decades × 2 reps = 96 libraries; QIIME2 + decontam analysis.
  • Estimated budget: ~$3,000–$4,000.
  • Target: Frontiers in Microbiology, ISME Communications, Environmental Microbiome.

ChatGPT
💬 Clarification triggered a meaningful protocol update Silica-gel–dried leaf tissue is a different (and in many ways better) beast than pressed sheets:
  • DNA integrity: markedly better → higher PCR success, smaller input masses needed.
  • Viable microbes: higher odds of reviving desiccation-tolerant fungi/bacteria.
  • Community profiles: silica drying preserves phyllosphere/endosphere signal better than pressing.
Updated surface sterilization protocol for brittle silica leaves (shorter EtOH/NaOCl exposures), added PVPP/PVP to lysis to mop polyphenols, and added accession ID chain-of-custody tracking with witness fragments.
Phase 4 — qPCR biomass SOP with TaqMan probes
You
[Requested the qPCR biomass SOP for silica-dried leaves]
ChatGPT
✓ Full SOP with volumes, thermoprofiles, acceptance criteria, and plate map

qPCR Biomass SOP Highlights (16S + Fungal ITS + IAC)

  • Input: 10 mg silica-dried leaf punch; KingFisher extraction with 1% PVP-40 in lysis.
  • Reaction: 20 µL total; 2× SYBR Master Mix; 0.3 µM primers; 1 µL template; 1 µL IAC (10&sup4; copies).
  • Thermoprofile: 95°C 2 min; 40 cycles (95°C 10 s → 60°C 30 s); melt curve.
  • Standards: 5-point 10-fold series (10&sup7;→10³ copies/µL); accept R² ≥ 0.99, efficiency 90–110%.
  • Inhibition: ΔCq(IAC) ≤ 1.0 = acceptable; > 1.0 = dilute template 1:5 and re-run.
  • Output: copies/mg = (copies/µL × elution volume) / tissue mass.
  • Triage rules: ≥10&sup5; copies/mg → culture priority; ≥10&sup4; copies/mg + clean blanks → amplicon shortlist.
Also provided a downloadable 96-well plate map CSV and metadata template.
You
I’m not using SYBR but instead TaqMan advanced probes for 16S and ITS.
ChatGPT
💬 Protocol updated for TaqMan probe chemistry TaqMan is ideal for low-biomass work (cleaner specificity, no melt-curve ambiguity). Updated the SOP with:
  • Duplex setup: Plate A (16S + IAC, FAM/Cy5); Plate B (Fungal + IAC, VIC/Cy5); optional triplex.
  • No melt curve; fast cycling: 95°C 2 min activation; 40 cycles (95°C 3 s → 60°C 30 s).
  • Double-quenched probes recommended for tighter baselines on degraded template.
  • IAC inhibition check unchanged; NTCs should show flat target channels with clean IAC amplification.
Phase 5 — Process mapping for Project 2
You
I’d like an overall process diagram outlining the main SOPs needing to be optimized for Project 2.
ChatGPT
✓ Generated process diagram for the silica-leaf endophyte workflow Produced a structured process map covering the five main SOP stages for Project 2:
  1. Sampling & chain-of-custody — 10–20 mg central lamina punch; accession ID recorded; witness fragment retained.
  2. qPCR pre-screen — KingFisher extraction; TaqMan 16S + fungal + IAC; rank by copies/mg and ΔCq; triage high-load samples forward.
  3. Surface sterilization — 70% EtOH 20–30 s → 0.5–1.0% NaOCl 45–60 s → sterile water rinse ×3; imprint last-rinse on PDA/TSA to verify surface kill.
  4. Culturing — Internal fragments on PDA, MEA, WA (fungi) and R2A, TSA (bacteria); 22–25°C, days 2–14; log morphotypes; cryobank at −80°C.
  5. Colony typing + Sanger ID — Boil preps; SYBR-qPCR triage (fungal vs. bacterial); PCR → Sanger (ITS or 16S); BLAST for putative genus/species.

What I took away

  • The initial question was deliberately open-ended, and ChatGPT organized the response by difficulty, cost, and novelty — exactly what I needed to prioritize. That structure came from the AI, not from me asking for it.
  • Supplying context iteratively (equipment, sample count, specimen type) meaningfully changed the output at each step. The silica-vs-pressed-sheet distinction triggered a real protocol update — not just a rephrasing.
  • Moving from SYBR to TaqMan was a one-sentence correction that generated a complete chemistry swap without losing any of the prior SOP structure. That kind of targeted editing without starting over is one of the most practically useful things about working in a persistent project.
  • The diagram-from-image failure was a real limitation — the tool couldn’t render from an attached hand-drawn flowchart. Being explicit about the fallback (vector-style, printable) kept the project moving.
  • The three project proposals came with estimated budgets, target journals, and CURE integration hooks built in. That framing — from specimen boxes to potential paper — in a single conversation is the core value here.
