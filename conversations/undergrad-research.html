Brainstorming Undergraduate Research with ChatGPT

ChatGPT Teaching Lab & Research Spring 2026
Context: I set up a ChatGPT project and uploaded past research data, sample inventories, and background materials on research-grade samples I have available for potential student use. I then used the project as a brainstorming partner to identify hypotheses with genuine peer-review publication potential — with students as coauthors. The conversation also covered experimental design and process mapping for experiments that could be run through course-based lab activities and outreach programs.
